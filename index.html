About this beta

QuietSocial is an offline focus app. It has no account system, Internet permission, advertisements, analytics SDK or developer-operated server. It does not send data to the developer automatically.

Accessibility access

With your separate consent and Android Accessibility access, QuietSocial reads foreground-window information and controls in supported social apps to apply the limits you choose. It uses Back or Home actions for deterministic feed, swipe, time and night rules. It does not stop you disabling Accessibility or uninstalling. Other chosen apps use foreground identification for time and night limits. YouTube video-title fingerprints may be compared transiently in memory to detect advances; neither raw titles nor fingerprints are saved.

Installed apps

Installed app identifiers and names are accessed locally for the chooser. Supported social app installation state and pending notices are stored to offer a limit when you install an app later. No limit is added automatically.

Information kept on your phone

Local storage includes chosen apps, settings, consent, counters, day locks, night plans, travel exceptions, test allowance and per-app notification markers. Counters roll over logically each local day; dated markers and removed-app settings may remain until app data is cleared. Install snapshots are replaced on scans, and notices are removed when acknowledged, an app is tracked, or the app is removed.

Optional diagnostics

Optional diagnostics are recorded only when you tap Record. One report contains sanitized control metadata, view IDs, classes, bounds, timing, quota state, app version, device model and Android version. Arbitrary messages, post text, video titles and screenshots are not intentionally stored in reports. A new recording replaces the old report; Delete report removes the stored copy. Stop and copy places the report on the Android clipboard. Sharing that copy with another person or app is your choice; their handling is outside QuietSocial. Deleting the local report does not remove exported copies.

Notifications

Optional local notifications cover approaching time limits, reached quotas, evening wake-up reminders and new social app notices. Android permission, channels and Do Not Disturb settings control their delivery. No remote push service is used.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored in Android app-private storage. The app does not add its own encryption layer. Cloud backup and device transfer of app data are disabled. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local app data, subject to Android behavior; it also resets limits. QuietSocial has no online account or account-deletion process.

Website hosting

Website hosting. The privacy webpage is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub receives the connection information needed to serve the page and logs visitors' IP addresses for security. The page contains no advertising or analytics scripts. Visiting the webpage is separate from QuietSocial's offline app operation. For GitHub's handling of website visitor information, see GitHub Privacy Statement

Free beta and emailed support reports

This beta build contains no advertising or payment SDK. All existing features remain available without payment. If you voluntarily email a diagnostic report to the developer, it is used to investigate your request and deleted within 30 days after resolution and no later than 90 days after receipt, whichever is earlier. Deleting the report on your phone does not delete a copy you emailed.

Required Usage Access and time protection

Accessibility and Usage Access are required to activate protection. You may decline or revoke access; protection then stays paused. Reliable time tracking uses Android Usage Access to read app foreground/background and screen-lock history locally. QuietSocial saves daily totals only for your selected apps. Today's earlier usage is included and may immediately exhaust a saved limit. This can recover missed time after a sampling interruption or process restart. A persistent protection notification supports continued enforcement when the dashboard is closed. No Internet, account, advertising SDK or automatic upload is added. Android permission revocation, Force stop and unavailable system history can still interrupt protection.
